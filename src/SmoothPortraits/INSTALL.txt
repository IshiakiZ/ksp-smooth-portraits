Smooth Portraits @VERSION@
for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x

The crew's portraits at the bottom right of the flight screen, drawn as often as the game itself instead of seven to ten times a second.
From 0.2.0 with smooth edges, and the crew's heads shaken by the ride (the g and the rumble), as in film from real cockpits.
From 0.3.0 a kerbal killed with their part has their last moments in their portrait: the cabin's camera catching the blow
and the fire, the picture tearing, and the static as the feed is lost.

INSTALL
  This mod needs Keystone, the base it stands on, which is a separate download:
    https://spacedock.info/mod/4665/Keystone
  Copy Keystone's folder (Keystone) into your KSP's GameData folder, and the SmoothPortraits
  folder from this download beside it, and start the game. Without Keystone the game does
  not load this mod. One copy of Keystone serves every mod that uses it. To remove this
  mod, delete the SmoothPortraits folder.

USE
  Open the Keystone window: the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Option-K on a
  Mac and Alt-K elsewhere. Then turn to Smooth Portraits (it is on as installed): the most
  pictures a second, the share of each frame they may take, kerbals outside the ship, and
  "Smooth edges", "Shaken by the ride" and "Last moments" (all on as installed).

WHERE IT WORKS
  Made and tried on a Mac; 0.1.2 was tried on Windows 11 too. What 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 add has
  been run on the Mac only so far (it is plain C#). Nobody has run it on Linux yet.

Everything else: https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-smooth-portraits
